## Maximalism

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `maximalism`
- **Cue:** Ornate layered motifs, intricate decorative patterns, elaborate linework, abundant overlapping forms, rich visual complexity

### Style
- Celebrate expressive abundance, visual multiplicity, and deliberate excess without losing the central idea.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use rich layered saturated colors, bright even light, and varied tactile surfaces.

### Shape
- Combine numerous distinctive forms and motifs in layered relationships that reward prolonged inspection.
