## Expired Film

- **Category:** Photo Technique
- **Style ID:** `expiredfilm`
- **Cue:** Expired film stock, shifted magenta-green colors, heavy grain, faded contrast, light leaks

### Apply
- Render the photograph as if shot on expired film stock: shifted magenta-green colors, heavy grain, faded contrast, fogged shadows, and occasional light leaks.
- Keep the chosen Photo Style, Color, and Tone; this technique only changes how the frame is captured.
