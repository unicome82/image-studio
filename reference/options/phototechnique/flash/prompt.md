## Flash

- **Category:** Photo Technique
- **Style ID:** `flash`
- **Cue:** Direct on-camera flash, crisp frontal light, hard shadow behind subject

### Apply
- Light the subject with direct on-camera flash: bright crisp frontal light, glossy specular highlights, a hard shadow edge behind, and a darker falloff background.
- Keep the chosen Photo Style, Color, and Tone; this technique only changes how the frame is captured.
