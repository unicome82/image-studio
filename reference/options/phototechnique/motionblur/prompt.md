## Motion Blur

- **Category:** Photo Technique
- **Style ID:** `motionblur`
- **Cue:** Slow shutter, directional motion streaks around one sharp anchor

### Apply
- Use a slow shutter so moving elements trail into smooth directional streaks while one anchor point stays sharp and readable.
- Keep the chosen Photo Style, Color, and Tone; this technique only changes how the frame is captured.
