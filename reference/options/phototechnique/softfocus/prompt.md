## Soft Focus

- **Category:** Photo Technique
- **Style ID:** `softfocus`
- **Cue:** Diffused glow, blooming highlights, gentle edges around a readable subject

### Apply
- Use a soft-focus diffusion look with glowing highlights, gentle edge softness, and lowered micro-contrast while the subject stays readable.
- Keep the chosen Photo Style, Color, and Tone; this technique only changes how the frame is captured.
