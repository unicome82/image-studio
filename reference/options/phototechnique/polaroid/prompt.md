## Polaroid

- **Category:** Photo Technique
- **Style ID:** `polaroid`
- **Cue:** Instant film print, soft focus, creamy muted colors, lifted blacks, white instant-print border

### Apply
- Render the photograph as an instant film print: soft focus, creamy muted colors, lifted blacks, gentle chemical unevenness, and a white instant-print border.
- Keep the chosen Photo Style, Color, and Tone; this technique only changes how the frame is captured.
