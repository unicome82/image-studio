## Flat

- **Category:** Depth
- **Style ID:** `flat`
- **Cue:** flattened space, frontal straight-on view, subject and background pressed close on one shallow plane, even scale throughout

### Apply
- Build the image on one shallow frontal plane so the subject, objects and background read side by side at a similar scale.
- Keep the viewpoint straight-on or directly overhead; in photographs use a frontal, flat-lay or compressed telephoto view.
