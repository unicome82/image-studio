## Layered

- **Category:** Depth
- **Style ID:** `layered`
- **Cue:** layered space, overlapping foreground, middle ground and background planes with moderate scale change

### Apply
- Stack the scene in distinct overlapping planes from foreground to background so depth reads through overlap and gradual scale change.
- Keep a moderate viewpoint; in photographs frame through a near element toward the subject and the background beyond.
