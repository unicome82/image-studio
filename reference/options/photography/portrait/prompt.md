## Portrait

- **Category:** Photo Style
- **Style ID:** `portrait`
- **Cue:** One person, eyes in focus, natural expression, real skin texture

### Apply
- Make a person the clear focus with flattering framing, natural expression, and attention to eyes, skin, and pose.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use natural true-to-life skin tones, soft window light, and clean, sharp detail.
