## Minimal

- **Category:** Photo Style
- **Style ID:** `minimal`
- **Cue:** Essential subjects isolated through precise framing, simplified geometric relationships, carefully controlled spacing, clean visual boundaries, subtle tonal transitions, restrained environmental detail, generous negative space, deliberate balance between subject and surrounding emptiness

### Apply
- Reduce the frame to one clear subject with generous empty space, simple shapes, and a calm, deliberate composition.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a restrained near-neutral palette, soft even light, and smooth clean surfaces.
