## Landscape

- **Category:** Photo Style
- **Style ID:** `landscape`
- **Cue:** Deep space, clear sense of place, atmospheric layers

### Apply
- Capture an expansive natural or urban scene with depth, a clear sense of place, and coherent atmospheric perspective.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use clear natural colors, soft golden or diffused daylight, and atmospheric depth.
