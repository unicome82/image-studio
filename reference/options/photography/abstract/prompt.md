## Abstract

- **Category:** Photo Style
- **Style ID:** `abstract`
- **Cue:** Close crops of form, line, pattern and light over literal subject

### Apply
- Isolate shapes, lines, reflections, and patterns from the subject so form and rhythm lead the image while it remains a real photograph.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a deliberate limited palette, sculpted directional light, and crisp surface detail.
