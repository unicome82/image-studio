## Editorial

- **Category:** Photo Style
- **Style ID:** `editorial`
- **Cue:** Intentional magazine-style composition, conceptual storytelling, art-directed subjects, refined styling, natural photographic detail

### Apply
- Use an intentional magazine-photography perspective, expressive subject direction, and a clear focal narrative.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a cohesive contemporary palette, soft directional light, and natural detail.
