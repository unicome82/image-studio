## Editorial

- **Category:** Photo Style
- **Style ID:** `editorial`
- **Cue:** Intentional magazine-style framing, art-directed subjects and environments, conceptual visual storytelling, refined yet natural styling, subtle imperfections in skin and materials, carefully balanced negative space, sophisticated compositional relationships

### Apply
- Use an intentional magazine-photography perspective, expressive subject direction, and a clear focal narrative.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a cohesive contemporary palette, soft directional light, and natural detail.
