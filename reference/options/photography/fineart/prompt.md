## Fine Art

- **Category:** Photo Style
- **Style ID:** `fineart`
- **Cue:** Sculptural compositions with carefully balanced volumes, conceptual visual relationships, subtle surface imperfections, tactile material textures, delicate shadow gradients, controlled spatial depth, contemplative visual atmosphere

### Apply
- Approach photography as a deliberate conceptual artwork with expressive framing and coherent visual intent.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a deliberate restrained palette, sculpted soft light, and subtle tonal detail.
