## Psychedelic Art

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `psychedelicart`
- **Cue:** Melting and swirling organic forms, interlocking fluid shapes, concentric rippling patterns, undulating hand-drawn contours, distorted spatial relationships, intricate flowing transitions, repeated wave-like structures, hypnotic visual rhythms

### Style
- Suggest altered perception, fluid transformation, and an immersive countercultural visual sensibility.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use vivid saturated complementary colors, glowing even light, and smooth flowing surfaces.

### Shape
- Distort familiar forms into undulating, melting, expanding, and interlocking shapes with perceptual ambiguity.
