## Storybook Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `storybookillustration`

### Style
- Create an evocative narrative image with the warmth and imaginative clarity of a picture book.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use fresh clear colors with clean whites, soft bright daylight, and a clean surface.

### Shape
- Give characters and objects readable gestures, distinctive personalities, and small narrative relationships that suggest a larger story.
