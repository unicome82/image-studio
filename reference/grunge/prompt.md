## Grunge

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `grunge`
- **Cue:** Rough gestural marks, distressed surfaces, torn edges, layered scratches and scuffs, deliberately imperfect textures

### Style
- Express anti-polished, subcultural visual energy with purposeful imperfection and visual friction.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use muted dark tones with one acid accent, harsh contrast light, and a gritty textured surface.

### Shape
- Use disrupted silhouettes, uneven structural relationships, and abrupt graphic interruptions that suggest an independent DIY attitude.
