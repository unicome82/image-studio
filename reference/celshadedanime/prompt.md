## Cel-shaded Anime

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `celshadedanime`

### Style
- Create a polished, contemporary cel-shaded animation look with expressive characters and vibrant, cinematic framing, without imitating any existing character or franchise.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use clear saturated colors with luminous skies, bright cinematic daylight, and a clean surface.

### Shape
- Use clean confident line work, expressive faces and poses, hard-edged two-tone or three-tone shadows, crisp highlights, and clearly separated color areas.
