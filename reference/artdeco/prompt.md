## Art Deco

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `artdeco`
- **Cue:** Stepped symmetry, sunburst and fan motifs, elongated forms, thin metallic linework

### Style
- Express the streamlined luxury and architectural elegance of Art Deco through disciplined ornament and rhythmic refinement.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a palette of deep navy, cream, and brushed gold, soft directional light, and a smooth clean surface.

### Shape
- Use stepped profiles, fan motifs, elongated forms, sunburst geometry, and deliberate repetition of stylized architectural shapes.
