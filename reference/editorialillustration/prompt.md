## Editorial Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `editorialillustration`
- **Cue:** One clever visual metaphor, few elements, conceptual twist as the focal point

### Style
- Interpret an idea as an insightful visual metaphor suitable for an editorial article or cultural publication.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a restrained palette of two or three confident hues on clean white, even light, and a smooth clean surface.

### Shape
- Translate abstract concepts into a single memorable visual relationship; simplify supporting elements around the conceptual focal point.
