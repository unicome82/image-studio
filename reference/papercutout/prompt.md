## Paper Cutout

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `papercutout`

### Style
- Interpret imagery as assembled shapes cut from physical sheets of paper, emphasizing the logic of overlapping cut pieces.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use clean bright paper colors with soft drop shadows, soft directional light, and a paper surface.

### Shape
- Use discrete silhouette pieces, clearly separated edges, and plausible layered cut shapes; simplify small internal details.
