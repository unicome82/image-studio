## Isometric Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `isometric`

### Style
- Present the scene as a clean, precise isometric construction with an orderly, diagrammatic clarity.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a clean palette of soft blues, whites, and one bright accent, even ambient light, and a smooth clean surface.

### Shape
- Build every object on a consistent isometric grid with parallel receding lines and equal axis angles; use crisp block-like volumes, readable top and side faces, and tidy spacing between elements.
