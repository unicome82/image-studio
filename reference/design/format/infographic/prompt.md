## Infographic

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `infographic`

### Instructions
- Create a finished infographic that explains one topic through a clear reading order, grouped information blocks, consistent icons or charts, and strong visual hierarchy. Keep data shapes simple and aligned on a systematic grid. Use only supplied wording and figures when typography is enabled; otherwise keep text areas unlettered. Do not invent statistics or show a presentation mockup.
