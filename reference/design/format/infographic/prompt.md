## Infographic

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `infographic`

### Instructions
- Create a finished infographic that explains one topic through a clear reading order, grouped information blocks, consistent icons or charts, and strong visual hierarchy. Keep data shapes simple and aligned on a systematic grid. When no text is rendered, convey the information through icons, charts, and unlettered placeholder bars. Do not invent statistics. Output the flat infographic itself as the full image.
