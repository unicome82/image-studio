## Pattern

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `pattern`

### Instructions
- Create a seamless repeating surface pattern with a balanced motif distribution, consistent scale, and no visible seams at the edges. Keep spacing rhythmic and the overall texture even across the canvas. Do not add borders or a single focal centerpiece; if text is rendered, repeat it as part of the motif. Output the flat pattern itself rather than a fabric, wallpaper, or product mockup.
