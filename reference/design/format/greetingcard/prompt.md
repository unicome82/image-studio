## Greeting Card

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `greetingcard`

### Instructions
- Create finished flat front artwork for a greeting or celebration card with a warm focal motif, clear hierarchy, and generous margins. Typeset any rendered message in a deliberate area; when no text is rendered, let the artwork fill that space instead of leaving an empty panel. Keep the mood suited to the occasion described in the subject. Do not show an envelope, table scene, or folded card mockup.
