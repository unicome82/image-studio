## Greeting Card

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `greetingcard`

### Instructions
- Create finished flat front artwork for a greeting or celebration card with a warm focal motif, clear hierarchy, and generous margins. Typeset any rendered message in a deliberate area; when no text is rendered, let the artwork fill that space. Keep the mood suited to the occasion described in the subject. Present the flat front artwork itself as the full image.
