## Greeting Card

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `greetingcard`

### Instructions
- Create finished flat front artwork for a greeting or celebration card with a warm focal motif, clear hierarchy, and generous margins. Reserve a deliberate area for the message; typeset only supplied wording when typography is enabled. Keep the mood suited to the occasion described in the subject. Do not show an envelope, table scene, or folded card mockup.
