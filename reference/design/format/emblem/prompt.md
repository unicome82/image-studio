## Emblem

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `emblem`

### Instructions
- Create a self-contained emblem or badge with an enclosed shape, a central symbol, and balanced surrounding ornament. Keep strong symmetry, a clear silhouette, and legibility at small sizes. Integrate any rendered text along the emblem structure. Avoid imitating existing crests or trademarks and do not show a patch, stamp, or merchandise mockup.
