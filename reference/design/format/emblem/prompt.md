## Emblem

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `emblem`
- **Cue:** Enclosed badge shape, central symbol, strong symmetry, reads at small size

### Instructions
- Create a self-contained emblem or badge with an enclosed shape, a central symbol, and balanced surrounding ornament. Keep strong symmetry, a clear silhouette, and legibility at small sizes. Integrate any rendered text along the emblem structure. Avoid imitating existing crests or trademarks. Present the flat emblem artwork itself on a plain background.
