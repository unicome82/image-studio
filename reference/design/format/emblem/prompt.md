## Emblem

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `emblem`

### Instructions
- Create a self-contained emblem or badge with an enclosed shape, a central symbol, and balanced surrounding ornament. Keep strong symmetry, a clear silhouette, and legibility at small sizes. If exact wording is requested and typography is enabled, integrate it along the emblem structure. Avoid imitating existing crests or trademarks and do not show a patch, stamp, or merchandise mockup.
