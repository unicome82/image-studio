## Logo

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `logo`

### Instructions
- Create a distinctive, scalable logo or symbol with a clear silhouette and balanced proportions. Keep small-size recognition and clean negative space. If exact brand wording is requested and typography is enabled, integrate it as a legible wordmark. Avoid imitating existing trademarks, watermarking, stationery mockups, or adding unsolicited slogans.
