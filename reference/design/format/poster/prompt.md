## Poster

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `poster`
- **Cue:** One dominant focal visual, strong hierarchy, deliberate margins

### Instructions
- Compose a finished poster with one clear focal point, deliberate margins, and a strong hierarchy readable at a distance. Balance the main visual with supporting negative space. When no text is rendered, let the visual fill that space. Present the flat poster artwork itself as the full image.
