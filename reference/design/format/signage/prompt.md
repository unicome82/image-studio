## Signage

- **Category:** Design Format
- **Style ID:** `signage`

### Instructions
- Create a finished flat sign or signage panel that communicates one message at a distance through a bold focal element, high contrast, and simple hierarchy. Keep generous margins and a clear reading direction. Typeset only supplied wording when typography is enabled; otherwise keep text areas unlettered. Do not show a storefront, wall, street scene, or installation mockup.
