## Luxury

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `luxury`
- **Cue:** Refined serif type, hairline rules, centered symmetry, rich restrained materials

### Instructions
- Create a premium, understated look: centered or calmly balanced layouts, refined high-contrast serif type, hairline rules, generous spacing, and rich but restrained material cues such as foil, embossing, or fine paper. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use deep black or navy with cream and a touch of brushed gold, soft directional light, and a fine smooth surface.
