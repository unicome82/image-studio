## Editorial

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `editorial`
- **Cue:** Magazine layout, oversized headline scale, strong image crops, airy columns

### Instructions
- Compose like a refined magazine spread: oversized headline scale against quiet body columns, confident image crops, generous margins, and a clear reading order. Pair a characterful serif with a clean sans-serif for any rendered text. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use warm off-white paper, black type, and one muted accent, soft even light, and a matte surface.
