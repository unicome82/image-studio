## Maximalism

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `maximalism`
- **Cue:** Dense layered elements, rich pattern, bold color, organized abundant hierarchy

### Instructions
- Fill the design with dense layered elements, rich patterns, bold color, and abundant decoration while keeping a clear hierarchy and one strong focal point. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use saturated jewel-like hues with warm accents, bright light, and a richly textured printed surface.
