## Maximalism

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `maximalism`
- **Cue:** Dense overlapping graphic elements, intricate decorative patterns, expressive typography, layered ornamental structures, repeated miniature motifs, complex visual rhythms, abundant surface detailing, carefully organized hierarchy within richly populated compositions

### Instructions
- Fill the design with dense, layered elements, rich patterns, and bold contrasting scales while a clear focal hierarchy keeps the abundance organized. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a saturated multi-hue palette with deep jewel accents, even light, and a clean surface.
