## Corporate

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `corporate`
- **Cue:** Clean structured grid, friendly sans-serif, clear icons, trustworthy balance

### Instructions
- Create a clean, trustworthy business look: structured grids, a friendly sans-serif hierarchy, clear icons and simple charts, consistent spacing, and calm balanced compositions. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use white with a confident blue and one supporting accent, even light, and a clean surface.
