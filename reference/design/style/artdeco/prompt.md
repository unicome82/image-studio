## Art Deco

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `artdeco`
- **Cue:** Stepped symmetry, sunburst and fan motifs, elongated geometric type, thin metallic rules

### Instructions
- Use a refined Art Deco graphic approach: strong central symmetry, stepped and tiered frames, sunburst and fan ornaments, elongated geometric forms, and thin parallel rules. Set any rendered text in tall, condensed geometric capitals with generous letter spacing. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use deep navy, cream, and brushed gold, soft directional light, and a smooth clean surface.
