## Anti-design

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `antidesign`
- **Cue:** Deliberately clashing layout, broken grid, raw default type, collage chaos

### Instructions
- Break conventions on purpose: clashing layouts, overlapping elements, broken grids, raw default-looking type, mismatched scales, and collage-like chaos that still reads as an intentional statement. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use harsh primaries with gray and neon accents, flat light, and a raw screen-like surface.
