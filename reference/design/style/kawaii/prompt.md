## Kawaii

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `kawaii`
- **Cue:** Rounded chubby forms, tiny faces, pastel softness, cheerful sticker-like charm

### Instructions
- Make the design cute and friendly: rounded chubby forms, small simple faces or characters, soft outlines, sticker-like elements, and cheerful bouncy rhythm. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use soft pastels with pink, mint, and butter yellow, gentle even light, and a smooth soft surface.
