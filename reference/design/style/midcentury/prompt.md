## Mid-century

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `midcentury`
- **Cue:** 1950s–60s inspired geometric and organic shapes, playful asymmetrical arrangements, tapered forms, simplified graphic motifs, distinctive modernist proportions, subtle vintage print textures, balanced relationships between curved and angular elements

### Instructions
- Use mid-century modern graphic language: organic and geometric shapes, flat layered color, playful asymmetric balance, and clean modern lettering when text appears. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use mustard, teal, burnt orange, and cream, even light, and a smooth clean surface.
