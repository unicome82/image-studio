## Acid

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `acid`
- **Cue:** Warped type, high-contrast distortion, liquid shapes, glitchy techno energy

### Instructions
- Push an acid graphics look: warped and stretched type, liquid chrome or blob shapes, high-contrast distortion, wireframe and glitch accents, and a restless techno energy that still holds one clear focus. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use black with acid green and electric violet, hard light, and a slick digital surface.
