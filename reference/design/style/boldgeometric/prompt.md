## Bold Geometric

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `boldgeometric`
- **Cue:** Large flat circles, arcs and blocks, tight alignment, strong contrast

### Instructions
- Build the design from large, confident geometric shapes such as circles, arcs, and blocks, with flat color, strong contrast, and tight alignment. Keep compositions simple and graphic, with a clear focal hierarchy and modern visual impact. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use two or three bold saturated hues with strong contrast, even light, and a flat clean surface.
