## Retro Graphic Design

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `retro`

### Instructions
- Use a coherent retro graphic vocabulary with period-inspired shapes, deliberate framing, bold hierarchy, and carefully balanced ornament. Add period texture only when Texture instructions are given. Follow the Color instructions when given; avoid fabricated dates, distressed unreadable lettering, and presentation mockups.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a fresh retro palette of warm red, cream, and teal with clean whites, even light, and a smooth clean surface.
