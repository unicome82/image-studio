## Memphis Design

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `memphis`
- **Cue:** Playful geometric shapes, squiggle accents, lively asymmetric balance

### Instructions
- Use playful geometric shapes, rhythmic pattern accents, contrasting scales, and lively asymmetric balance inspired by Memphis graphics. Keep a clear focal hierarchy and controlled spacing so pattern does not overwhelm the message. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use bright primary and pastel accents with black details on white, even light, and a clean surface.
