## Flat

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `flat`
- **Cue:** Solid flat color shapes, simple icon-like forms, crisp edges, zero depth

### Instructions
- Build the design from solid flat color shapes with crisp edges and simplified, icon-like forms. Keep every surface flat and even, showing depth only through overlap and color, and let color and shape carry the hierarchy. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a bright limited palette of four to five clear hues, even light, and a smooth clean surface.
