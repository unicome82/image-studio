## Organic

- **Category:** Design Style
- **Style ID:** `organic`
- **Cue:** Soft flowing curves, hand-touched shapes, natural materials, gentle irregular rhythm

### Instructions
- Shape the design with soft flowing curves, hand-touched irregular forms, and natural material cues such as paper fiber, clay, linen, or leaves. Let the layout breathe with a gentle, uneven rhythm. Follow the Color instructions when given.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use warm earthy neutrals with sage and terracotta, soft daylight, and a natural paper surface.
