## Risograph

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `risograph`
- **Cue:** Layered spot-color ink printing, subtle color misregistration, grainy ink textures, uneven ink coverage, visible paper grain

### Style
- Recreate the character of stencil-based risograph printing: layered flat ink deposits with decisive boundaries, slight plate misregistration, and uneven ink coverage.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use two to four bright soy-ink colors such as blue, fluorescent pink, yellow, and orange on warm off-white paper.

### Shape
- Use simple, readable shapes built from overlapping flat ink layers; let a few areas overprint to create new tones.
