## Outline Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `outlineillustration`
- **Cue:** Clean contour lines of even weight, forms left unfilled on open background

### Style
- Express the subject entirely through clean, deliberate outlines and essential interior linework, like a refined line-art illustration.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use one dark line color on bright white, even light, and a clean surface.

### Shape
- Define silhouettes and key inner boundaries with confident contours; simplify forms to their readable edges and leave generous open space.

### Line
- Keep a steady, even line weight with rounded ends; vary it only slightly to separate the main silhouette from inner details.

### Color
- Leave forms unfilled; apply any chosen palette to the lines themselves, giving each line color a clear role.

### Texture
- Keep lines crisp and the background flat unless a texture is requested.

### Typography
- When text is requested, draw it as outlined or single-stroke letters that match the line weight.
