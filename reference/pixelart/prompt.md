## Pixel Art

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `pixelart`
- **Cue:** Strict low-resolution pixel-grid construction, hard stepped contours, carefully clustered square pixels, deliberate pixel placement, block-based silhouettes, pixel-level shading, patterned dithering, consistent pixel scale

### Style
- Create deliberate pixel art with the clarity and charm of contemporary independent game art.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a limited bright palette with clear contrast, even light, and crisp pixel edges.

### Shape
- Construct every form on a visible square pixel grid with clean stepped edges, a limited palette, intentional single-pixel details, and readable silhouettes; keep pixels sharp and uniformly sized.
