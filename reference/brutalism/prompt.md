## Brutalism

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `brutalism`

### Style
- Express raw, uncompromising visual directness with deliberately exposed structural decisions.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use stark black, white, and one loud accent, hard frontal light, and a raw flat surface.

### Shape
- Use blunt masses, hard interruptions, forceful scale changes, and unapologetic visual tension.
