## Botanical Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `botanicalillustration`

### Style
- Convey the distinctive morphology and growth logic of plants through careful observational interpretation.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with a crisp finish, clean bright whites, and true-to-hue colors rather than an aged, yellowed, or sepia patina.

### Shape
- Represent identifiable stems, leaves, petals, seed structures, and branching patterns with coherent botanical relationships.
