## Botanical Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `botanicalillustration`
- **Cue:** Accurate plant anatomy, fine observational linework, specimen-style layout on open paper

### Style
- Convey the distinctive morphology and growth logic of plants through careful observational interpretation.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use fresh natural greens with clear floral accents on bright white, soft even daylight, and a clean paper surface.

### Shape
- Represent identifiable stems, leaves, petals, seed structures, and branching patterns with coherent botanical relationships.
