## Mid-century Modern

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `midcentury`
- **Cue:** Abstracted organic shapes, boomerang curves, flat textured fills, playful asymmetry

### Style
- Interpret subjects through the optimistic, design-conscious visual language of mid-twentieth-century modernism.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use fresh mustard, teal, coral, and cream on clean white, even light, and a clean surface.

### Shape
- Favor confident abstracted silhouettes, playful organic geometry, and restrained asymmetrical relationships between forms.
