## Expressionism

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `expressionism`

### Style
- Prioritize subjective feeling and psychological intensity over objective description.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with a crisp finish, clean bright whites, and true-to-hue colors rather than an aged, yellowed, or sepia patina.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use bold emotional contrasts of saturated color, strong directional light, and visible energetic brushwork.

### Shape
- Stretch, compress, and angularly distort figures or environments to convey inner tension and expressive force.
