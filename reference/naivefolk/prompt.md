## Naive Folk Art

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `naivefolk`
- **Cue:** Flattened frontal perspective, decorative repeated motifs, ornamental borders, opaque hand-painted gouache, visible brush texture

### Style
- Naive folk art inspired by traditional folk paintings and self-taught artistic expression. Emphasize instinctive, unconventional, deliberately unrefined interpretation; preserve playful imagination, innocent charm, and subtle visual humor.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use bright cheerful folk colors on a clean light ground, even daylight, and a clean surface.

### Shape
- Fundamentally distort the structure and proportions of subjects, not merely their surface appearance. Simplify recognizable objects into primitive, irregular, organic forms. Exaggerate, stretch, compress, or displace features into unexpected silhouettes; embrace awkward anatomy and imperfect spatial relationships while preserving recognition.
