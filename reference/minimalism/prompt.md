## Minimalism

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `minimalism`

### Style
- Reduce the visual concept to its irreducible essentials while retaining clear meaning and intentional presence.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a near-white base with one quiet accent hue, soft even light, and a smooth clean surface.

### Shape
- Use a small number of decisive forms and purposeful relationships; remove every element that does not support recognition or concept.
