## Cubism

- **Category:** Base Style
- **Style ID:** `cubism`

### Style
- Represent multiple viewpoints simultaneously rather than depicting a single optical perspective.

### Shape
- Fragment recognizable subjects into intersecting planar facets; rotate, flatten, and recombine structural parts while retaining conceptual identity.

### Avoid
- Avoid conventional one-point realism, seamless anatomy, and merely applying polygon filters to an unchanged subject.
