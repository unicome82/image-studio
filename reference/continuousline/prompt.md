## Continuous Line

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `continuousline`
- **Cue:** One unbroken line of steady weight drawing the whole subject; open loops

### Style
- Express the subject with the elegance and economy of a single uninterrupted line drawn with confident flow.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use a single dark line on bright white with one or two soft flat color shapes, even light, and a clean surface.

### Shape
- Describe contours with one continuous stroke of steady weight that loops and overlaps naturally; keep forms recognizable with minimal lines and generous open space, adding only a few flat color shapes when needed.
