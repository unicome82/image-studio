## Pop Art

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `popart`
- **Cue:** Bold graphic contours, Ben-Day halftone dot patterns, sharply simplified iconic forms, comic-book printing textures, repeated graphic motifs, hard-edged shape divisions, dramatic cropping, deliberate separation of printed color areas

### Style
- Treat familiar consumer imagery and mass-culture symbols as striking graphic icons with playful irony.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use bold primary colors with black outlines on white, flat bright light, and a clean printed surface.

### Shape
- Simplify objects into emphatic recognizable emblems; exaggerate commercial iconography and repeated motifs.
