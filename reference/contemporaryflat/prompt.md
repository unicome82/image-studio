## Contemporary Flat Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `contemporaryflat`

### Style
- Create a fresh, present-day flat illustration with confident simplification, crisp edges, and a polished digital finish suited to modern brands and interfaces.

### Shape
- Use simplified geometric bodies, smooth rounded silhouettes, stylized proportions, and minimal interior detail; layer clean overlapping planes with subtle tonal shapes instead of heavy rendering.
