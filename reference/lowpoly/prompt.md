## Low Poly

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `lowpoly`

### Style
- Render the scene as a clean low-polygon construction with a contemporary digital-faceted aesthetic.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use cool gradients of related hues, soft directional light that reveals each facet, and a smooth clean surface.

### Shape
- Build forms from visible triangular facets with crisp edges; express volume through flat-shaded face planes of related tones and keep silhouettes simple and readable.
