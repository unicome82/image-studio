## Y2K

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `y2k`

### Style
- Reference the optimistic, synthetic, futuristic visual imagination of the late 1990s and early 2000s.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with a crisp finish, clean bright whites, and true-to-hue colors rather than an aged, yellowed, or sepia patina.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use glossy chrome, icy blue, and bubblegum pink, bright studio light, and glossy reflective surfaces.

### Shape
- Use inflated or aerodynamic forms, playful techno-organic silhouettes, and exaggerated digital-age iconography.
