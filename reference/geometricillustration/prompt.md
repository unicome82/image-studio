## Geometric Illustration

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `geometricillustration`

### Style
- Translate recognizable subjects into an intentionally constructed language of geometric relationships.

### Shape
- Build forms from circles, polygons, arcs, and precise intersections; retain identity through proportion and silhouette rather than realistic surface modeling.

### Avoid
- Avoid accidental organic distortion, unnecessary realism, and inconsistent geometric rules.
