## Bauhaus

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `bauhaus`
- **Cue:** Circles, squares and bars on a rational grid; every form purely structural

### Style
- Apply functionalist Bauhaus visual thinking: every element should have a clear structural purpose; emphasize the relationship between elementary form and practical clarity.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use primary red, yellow, and blue on clean white, even flat light, and a clean surface.

### Shape
- Construct subjects from circles, squares, triangles, bars, and rationally interlocking geometric masses; reduce ornamental contours to essential structure.
