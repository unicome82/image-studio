## Impressionism

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `impressionism`

### Style
- Capture fleeting perception and the immediacy of a passing moment rather than exact representation.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with a crisp finish, clean bright whites, and true-to-hue colors rather than an aged, yellowed, or sepia patina.

### Shape
- Allow subject boundaries to feel optically transient and loosely resolved; emphasize relationships between perceived masses.
