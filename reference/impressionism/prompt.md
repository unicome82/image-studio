## Impressionism

- **Category:** Illustration Style
- **Style ID:** `impressionism`
- **Cue:** Broken dabs of color, visible painterly brushstrokes, softly dissolving edges, optical mixing of light and color

### Style
- Capture fleeting perception and the immediacy of a passing moment rather than exact representation.
- Interpret it as a fresh contemporary work made today, with clean bright whites and true-to-hue colors.
- When no Color, Lighting, or Texture instructions are given, use fresh bright colors with clean whites, bright natural daylight, and visible light brushwork.

### Shape
- Allow subject boundaries to feel optically transient and loosely resolved; emphasize relationships between perceived masses.
